# Background

The Background pages explain the conventions and models behind Larmor's results. Read them to check your data
against what Larmor expects, or to know what a computation does.

- [Conventions](conventions.md) gives the coordinates, gradient table frames, spherical harmonic bases, tractogram
  files, array axes and units that Larmor uses, and how the viewer displays images.
- [Simulating diffusion MRI](simulation.md) describes the signal the simulation computes, from compartments and
  tissues to relaxation and noise, and what it does not model.

The papers behind each method are listed under [Citing](../about/citing.md#methods-and-papers).
