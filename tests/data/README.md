# Test reference data

The tests compare Larmor with results from other tools. Those results are stored here, small enough to commit (at most
1 MB per file), so the tests run on every machine and in CI without the tools themselves.

## Sources and licences

Every file here is either synthetic or made from one of two openly licensed datasets:

- **Synthetic**: made by the scripts named in the table from numbers alone (numpy, or MATLAB for `matlab/`), and the
  tools' outputs on them: `sh_tournier_ref.npz`, `grl_kurtosis_kernel.mat`, `mif/`, `tck/`, `vtk/`, `tracking/`,
  `registration/`, `matlab/`, `gibbs/phantom.nii.gz`, `editing/edge_cases*.tck`, `editing/weights.txt`,
  `tsf/edges.*`, `connectome/template.nii.gz` and `connectome/weights.txt`. These are part of Larmor, under its
  licence.
- **CFIN multi-shell** (Hansen B, Jespersen SN. Data for evaluation of fast kurtosis strategies, b-value optimization
  and exploration of diffusion MRI contrast. Scientific Data 3 (2016) 160072,
  https://doi.org/10.1038/sdata.2016.72), released under
  [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/) and fetched by `larmor.data.fetch_cfin_multishell`:
  `dki/`, `gibbs/dwi.nii.gz` and the `dwi` part of `gibbs/mrdegibbs_ref.npz`, `mritoolkit/`, `wm_rf/`, `mfod/`,
  `msmt/`, and the kernel problems of `lasso/lasso_problems.mat`. These are crops of the data and results computed from them.
- **ISMRM 2015 Tractography Challenge** (Maier-Hein KH, Neher PF, Houde J-C, et al. The challenge of mapping the
  human connectome based on diffusion tractography. Nature Communications 8 (2017) 1349,
  https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-017-01285-x; data at https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.572345), released under
  [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/) and fetched by `larmor.data.fetch_ismrm2015`:
  `grl/`, `grl_obj.mat`, `deconv_obj.mat`, `leemans_tournier_ref.mat`, `bvals.txt`, `bvecs.txt`, `bmat.txt`,
  `peaks/`, `asym/`, `csd/`, `editing/` (except the synthetic files above), `tsf/bzero*` and `connectome/` (except the
  synthetic files above). These are changed from the original: cropped, and processed into FODs, tractograms, a
  parcellation and the tools' results on them, as the table describes. The intermediate files they come from (the
  MRtrix FOD `fod.nii.gz`, MRIToolkit's `fods_mrtrix.nii.gz`, the tractogram `MRT/tracks.tck` and the parcellation
  `fake_parcellation.nii.gz` with its `.json`) were themselves made from the challenge data, so they and everything
  made from them are under the same licence.
