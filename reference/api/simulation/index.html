class larmor.simulation.Bundle(source, tissue='WM', path=None, streamline_area=None, densest_fill=0.7)#

A fibre bundle of a phantom, and the tissue that fills it.

A bundle places its tissue in the voxels it passes through, and its streamlines give the fibre directions there. The share of a voxel that a bundle fills is its occupancy, between 0 and 1. A bundle drawn from a geometry fills each voxel by the share of the voxel inside its tube. A tractogram, or a geometry with a streamline_area, fills each voxel with L a / V, capped at 1, where L is the length of its streamlines in the voxel (mm), a the cross-section each streamline stands for (mm^2) and V the voxel volume (mm^3).

Parameters:

  • source (BundleGeometry | Tractogram) – A geometry to draw the streamlines from, or a tractogram (for example from load_tractogram), in world coordinates (mm).

  • tissue (str) – The name of the tissue that fills the bundle. Defaults to “WM”.

  • path (Path | None) – The file a tractogram source was read from; write_phantom stores it. Defaults to None.

  • streamline_area (float | None) – The cross-section a that each streamline stands for, in mm^2. Defaults to None, with which a geometry fills its tube and a tractogram gets the value that gives the bundle’s dense voxels an occupancy of densest_fill. With a value, a geometry’s occupancy dips at random where fewer streamlines happen to pass.

  • densest_fill (float) – The occupancy of a tractogram bundle’s dense voxels when streamline_area is None, in (0, 1]. A voxel is dense when the bundle’s streamline length in it reaches the 95th percentile of that length over the voxels the bundle reaches; denser voxels are capped at 1. Not used for a geometry. Defaults to 0.7.

Raises:

ValueError – If streamline_area is not positive or densest_fill is not in (0, 1].

streamlines(index=0)#

Returns the streamlines, drawn from the geometry or the tractogram.

Parameters:

index (int) – The bundle’s index, stored in streamline_data[“bundle”] of the streamlines drawn from a geometry. Defaults to 0.

Returns:

The streamlines in world coordinates (mm).

Return type:

Tractogram

class larmor.simulation.BundleGeometry(control_points, num_streamlines=1000, radius=2.0, num_points=100, fan_start=0.0, fan_end=0.0, fan_sharpness=2.0, dispersion=0.0, seed=None)#

The shape of a synthetic bundle.

A geometry describes a bundle by a few control points along its centreline, a radius, and how much the bundle fans out at its ends. Each streamline is a spline through the control points, shifted across the centreline by an offset drawn uniformly in a disc of radius. The offset is scaled by 1 + fan_start (1 - u)^p + fan_end u^p, where u in [0, 1] is the position along the centreline and p is fan_sharpness. A random wander of standard deviation dispersion is added at the inner control points.

Parameters:

  • control_points (ndarray) – (K, 3) points the centreline passes through, K >= 2, in world coordinates (RAS+) in mm.

  • num_streamlines (int) – The number of streamlines. Defaults to 1000.

  • radius (float) – The bundle’s radius in mm. Defaults to 2.0.

  • num_points (int) – Points per streamline. Defaults to 100.

  • fan_start (float) – Extra spread at the first control point, where the disc has the radius radius (1 + fan_start). Defaults to 0.0.

  • fan_end (float) – Extra spread at the last control point, in the same way. Defaults to 0.0.

  • fan_sharpness (float) – p, how close to the ends the fanning stays; a larger p keeps it nearer the ends. Defaults to 2.0.

  • dispersion (float) – Standard deviation of the wander, mm. Defaults to 0.0.

  • seed (int | None) – The seed of the random offsets and wander, so that the same seed gives the same streamlines; None draws new ones each time. Defaults to None.

Raises:

ValueError – If the control points are not (K, 3) finite values with K >= 2, or a count or size is out of range.

class larmor.simulation.Compartment(model, fraction, diffusivity, *, t1_ms, t2_ms, t2star_ms, proton_density=1.0, perpendicular_diffusivity=0.0, radius_um=0.0, kappa=0.0)#

One water pool of a tissue, with its diffusion model, fraction and relaxation.

A tissue is a sum of compartments, such as the water inside axons and the water around them. The models and their attenuation for b-value b (s/mm^2), unit gradient direction g and unit fibre direction n are:

  • STICK: exp(-b D (g.n)^2), diffusion along the fibre only (Behrens et al. 2003).

  • ZEPPELIN: exp(-b (D_perp + (D - D_perp) (g.n)^2)), an axially symmetric tensor (Kaden et al. 2016, Eq. 5; Novikov et al. 2019, Eq. 3.5). D_perp > D is allowed (an oblate tensor), although white matter has D_perp < D.

  • BALL: exp(-b D), free isotropic diffusion (Novikov et al. 2019, Eq. 3.5).

  • POWDER_STICK: sqrt(pi) / (2 sqrt(b D)) erf(sqrt(b D)), the stick averaged over all fibre directions (Kroenke et al. 2004; Kaden et al. 2016).

  • SPHERE: diffusion restricted to an impermeable sphere of radius R, in the Gaussian phase approximation for rectangular pulses, as SANDI uses for the soma (Palombo et al. 2020, Eq. 8). It depends on the pulse timings, for a gradient echo those of the bipolar pair that Protocol describes.

  • WATSON_STICK: sticks of diffusivity D whose directions spread about the fibre direction with the Watson density f(n) = exp(kappa (mu.n)^2) / M(1/2, 3/2, kappa), the neurite compartment of NODDI (Zhang et al. 2012). kappa = 0 gives the powder-averaged stick, and a large kappa the stick.

  • CYLINDER: diffusion restricted to an impermeable cylinder of radius R along the fibre, in the Gaussian phase approximation for rectangular pulses across it (Van Gelderen et al. 1994; De Santis et al. 2016, Eq. 3) and free with D along it. It depends on the pulse timings.

  • DOT: a signal of 1 at every b-value, for stationary water trapped in glial cells and other small structures (Panagiotaki et al. 2012). D is unused.

  • FREE_WATER: exp(-b D), a ball that the ground truth counts as free water, as in free-water imaging (Pasternak et al. 2009).

A compartment’s signal at b = 0 is PD (1 - exp(-TR / T1)) exp(-TE / T2) for a spin echo, with T2* in place of T2 for a gradient echo.

Parameters:

  • model (CompartmentModel) – The diffusion model.

  • fraction (float) – The compartment’s share of its tissue’s volume, in [0, 1].

  • diffusivity (float) – D in mm^2/s; for the stick and the zeppelin, the diffusivity along the fibre.

  • t1_ms (float) – T1 in ms.

  • t2_ms (float) – T2 in ms.

  • t2star_ms (float) – T2* in ms, at most T2.

  • proton_density (float) – The water content relative to pure water, in [0, 1]. Defaults to 1.0.

  • perpendicular_diffusivity (float) – The zeppelin’s D_perp across the fibre, in mm^2/s. Defaults to 0.0; only the zeppelin uses it.

  • radius_um (float) – The radius of the sphere or the cylinder in um. Defaults to 0.0; only the sphere and the cylinder use it.

  • kappa (float) – The Watson concentration of WATSON_STICK, >= 0. Defaults to 0.0; only WATSON_STICK uses it.

Raises:

ValueError – If the model is unknown or a value is out of range.

class larmor.simulation.Phantom(grid, tissues=<factory>, bundles=(), tissue_maps=(), background=None)#

An object to simulate, made of tissues placed on a voxel grid.

Each voxel is split between the tissues in three steps, which never scale a fraction up. Each bundle first takes its occupancy; where the bundles add up to more than 1, they are scaled down to sum to 1. The tissue maps then share what the bundles leave, scaled down where they add up to more. The rest goes to the background tissue, or gives no signal when background is None, so a voxel with a grey matter map value of 0.05 and nothing else is 5% grey matter and 95% background.

Parameters:

  • grid (Grid) – The voxel grid.

  • tissues (tuple[Tissue, ...]) – The named tissues. Defaults to WM, GM and CSF (default_tissues).

  • bundles (tuple[Bundle, ...]) – The fibre bundles. Defaults to none.

  • tissue_maps (tuple[TissueMap, ...]) – At most one map per tissue. Defaults to none.

  • background (str | None) – The name of the tissue that fills what remains, or None for no signal. Its compartments must not need a fibre direction. Defaults to None.

Raises:

ValueError – If tissue names repeat, a bundle, map or the background names an unknown tissue, a map or the background uses a tissue that needs a fibre direction, two maps share a tissue, or a map is not a 3D volume on the grid with values in [0, 1].

Parameters:

Returns:

The tissue.

Raises:

ValueError – If the phantom has no tissue of that name.

Return type:

class larmor.simulation.Protocol(gradients, small_delta_ms, big_delta_ms, te_ms, tr_ms, sequence=PulseSequence.SPIN_ECHO)#

A diffusion-weighted scan: the gradient table and the pulse timings.

Every simulation engine takes a protocol with the phantom. The diffusion weighting is a pair of rectangular gradient pulses (pulsed gradient spin echo), so b = gamma^2 G^2 delta^2 (Delta - delta / 3). A gradient echo has no refocusing pulse, so it is assumed to use a bipolar pair instead: a lobe of +G, then one of -G, each delta long and Delta apart. Its effective waveform is the same, and so are its b-value and its restricted signal.

The protocol keeps its own read-only copy of the gradient table, so change a table before building a protocol from it, not after.

Parameters:

  • gradients (GradientTable) – The b-value (s/mm^2) and direction of each volume.

  • small_delta_ms (float) – delta, the duration of each gradient pulse, in ms.

  • big_delta_ms (float) – Delta, the time between the starts of the two pulses, in ms.

  • te_ms (tuple[float, ...]) – The echo time, or a sequence of echo times for a multi-echo acquisition, in ms. Every echo shares the one diffusion weighting, which comes before the first echo.

  • tr_ms (float) – The repetition time, in ms.

  • sequence (PulseSequence) – SPIN_ECHO (relaxation with T2) or GRADIENT_ECHO (with T2*). Defaults to SPIN_ECHO.

Raises:

ValueError – If a timing is not positive and finite, delta > Delta, Delta + delta >= the first TE, or TR <= the last TE.

Creates a multi-shell gradient table with evenly spread directions.

Use it to build the gradient table of a Protocol. Each shell’s directions start at random and are spread by electrostatic repulsion on the half sphere: every direction and its opposite carry a charge, and projected gradient descent lowers the energy, the sum over pairs of 1 / norm(x_i - x_j) + 1 / norm(x_i + x_j). Returns:

larmor.simulation.noddi_tissue(*, name='WM', intra_fraction=0.6, free_water_fraction=0.05, kappa=8.0, diffusivity=0.0017, free_water_diffusivity=0.003, t1_ms=832.0, t2_ms=69.0, t2star_ms=46.55, proton_density=0.7)#

Makes a NODDI white matter tissue: Watson sticks, zeppelin and free water.

The neurites are sticks of diffusivity d dispersed by a Watson density of concentration kappa (Zhang et al. 2012, Eqs. 2 and 3), and the extra-neurite space is a zeppelin whose diffusivities follow from d by the tortuosity model averaged over the same density (Eqs. 4 to 6, with tau1 from Eq. 7). Free water has the relaxation of free_water_tissue, apart from T2*. The orientation dispersion index is OD = (2 / pi) arctan(1 / kappa) (Eq. 9).

intra_fraction (v_ic) and free_water_fraction (v_iso) are volume fractions. A NODDI fit recovers signal fractions instead, which differ with the defaults because free water has a higher proton density and a longer T2.

The sticks and the zeppelin share T1, T2, T2* and proton density; the free water shares the tissue’s T2’ = 1 / (1/T2* - 1/T2) and gets T2*_fw = 1 / (1/T2_fw + 1/T2’). The relaxation defaults assume 3 T.

Sources of the defaults:

  • diffusivity 1.7e-3 and free_water_diffusivity 3.0e-3 mm^2/s: the values NODDI fixes (Zhang et al. 2012).

  • intra_fraction 0.6, free_water_fraction 0.05 and kappa 8 (OD about 0.08): choices for coherent white matter.

  • T1 832 ms and T2* 46.6 ms: white matter at 3 T in vivo (Wansapura et al. 1999); T2 69 ms: frontal white matter at 3 T in vivo (Lu et al. 2005, Table 1).

  • proton_density 0.7: an approximate choice for white matter water content (source to be added).

Parameters:

  • name (str) – The tissue’s name. Defaults to “WM”.

  • intra_fraction (float) – v_ic, the neurites’ share of the tissue outside free water. Defaults to 0.6.

  • free_water_fraction (float) – v_iso, the free-water share. Defaults to 0.05.

  • kappa (float) – The Watson concentration, >= 0. Defaults to 8.

  • diffusivity (float) – d, the diffusivity along the neurites, mm^2/s. Defaults to 1.7e-3.

  • free_water_diffusivity (float) – The free water’s D, mm^2/s. Defaults to 3.0e-3.

  • t1_ms (float) – T1 of the sticks and the zeppelin, ms. Defaults to 832.

  • t2_ms (float) – T2 of the sticks and the zeppelin, ms. Defaults to 69.

  • t2star_ms (float) – T2* of the sticks and the zeppelin, ms. Defaults to 46.6.

  • proton_density (float) – The proton density of the sticks and the zeppelin. Defaults to 0.7.

Returns:

The tissue, with free water only if free_water_fraction > 0.

Raises:

ValueError – If a fraction is outside [0, 1] or a value is out of range.

larmor.simulation.save_simulation(simulated, folder, *, force=False, bundle_names=None)#

Writes a simulation and its ground truth into one self-contained folder.

The folder holds the DWI with its .bval and .bvec in the FSL frame, the ground truth, the bundles’ streamlines, the phantom file with the scan and simulation.json, as SimulationFiles lists them. Running the phantom file again with the seed recorded in simulation.json gives the same images.

The folder is created if needed. If it holds an earlier simulation, the files that its simulation.json lists are removed first. A folder that holds other files is refused unless force is True, which then writes into it and keeps them.

The ground-truth peaks come from the voxel’s own fibres, not from a fit: fibres within 20 degrees of a larger one join its peak, a direction and its opposite counting as the same, and peaks below 10% of the voxel’s largest are dropped. Each peak’s direction is the weighted mean of its fibres. A tissue mask holds the voxels where the tissue, background included, fills at least half.

The tissue, bundle, compartment and free-water fractions are volume fractions. A model fitted to the images recovers signal fractions, which differ because each compartment’s signal is weighted by its proton density and relaxation: at TE 80 ms and TR 3000 ms, the NODDI preset’s free-water volume fraction of 0.05 is about 0.11 of the b = 0 signal. Compare a fit with the signal fractions, which are written too.

Parameters:

  • simulated (SimulatedDWI) – The result of simulate.

  • folder (str | Path) – The output folder.

  • force (bool) – Whether to write into a folder that is not empty and holds no simulation.json. Defaults to False.

  • bundle_names (Sequence[str] | None) – A name for each bundle, kept in the phantom file for the viewer, such as PhantomFile.bundle_names of the file the phantom was read from. Defaults to None, which leaves the bundles numbered.

Returns:

The paths written.

Raises:

  • FileExistsError – If the folder is not empty, has no simulation.json and force is False.

  • ValueError – If simulated does not carry its phantom and protocol (it was not made by simulate), two tissue names give the same file name, or bundle_names does not name every bundle.

Return type:

SimulationFiles

larmor.simulation.simulate(phantom, protocol, *, snr=None, noise_reference='WM', seed=None, n_jobs=-1, progress=None)#

Simulates the diffusion-weighted images of a phantom, voxel by voxel.

The signal of each compartment in each voxel has a closed form, and the voxel’s signal is their sum, as if the whole acquisition happened at once: there is no k-space, readout, distortion or motion. Each compartment counts with its fraction of the voxel and its relaxation weight, with T2* in place of T2 for a gradient echo; an oriented compartment is evaluated along each fibre in the voxel, weighted by the fibre’s share. The result does not depend on n_jobs.

With snr, Rician noise is added with sigma = S0_ref / snr, where S0_ref is the b = 0 signal, at the first echo time, of a voxel filled with the noise_reference tissue.

Parameters:

  • phantom (Phantom) – The object to simulate.

  • protocol (Protocol) – The scan.

  • snr (float | None) – The signal-to-noise ratio of S0_ref. Defaults to None, no noise.

  • noise_reference (str) – The name of the tissue that defines S0_ref. Defaults to “WM”.

  • seed (int | None) – The seed of the noise and of the bundle geometries that have no seed of their own (see partition_voxels); the same seed gives the same result. Defaults to None, which draws a seed and returns it in the result.

  • n_jobs (int) – The number of threads, -1 for all cores. Defaults to -1.

  • progress (Callable[[int, int, str], None] | None) – A function called with (done, total, stage) as the run goes on, where stage is “voxelise”, “signal” or “noise”; raise an exception in it to cancel the run. Defaults to None.

Returns:

The images, the noise-free images and the ground truth.

Raises:

ValueError – If snr is not positive, the noise reference is not a tissue of the phantom, or n_jobs is 0 or below -1.

Return type:

  • extra_parallel_diffusivity (float) – The zeppelin’s D along the fibre, mm^2/s. Defaults to 1.7e-3.

larmor.simulation.write_phantom(phantom, path, *, protocol=None, viewer=None)#

Writes a phantom file (JSON, suggested suffix .phantom.json).

Tractograms and tissue maps are stored as paths, both absolute and relative to the file’s folder. A bundle geometry without a seed gets one drawn at random and written, so the file always gives the same streamlines.

Parameters:

  • phantom (Phantom) – The phantom.

  • path (str | Path) – The file to write.

  • protocol (Protocol | None) – The scan to store with it. Defaults to None.

  • viewer (Mapping[str, Any] | None) – The viewer’s settings, any JSON-ready values. Defaults to None.

Raises:

ValueError – If a tractogram bundle or a tissue map has no path.
