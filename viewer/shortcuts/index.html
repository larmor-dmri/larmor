Keyboard shortcuts#

This page lists every key and mouse control of the viewer, as Help > Shortcuts lists them in the viewer itself. Ctrl is Cmd on macOS. A key acts on the selected view, which is the view you clicked last, and works only when that view is one of those listed for the key.
