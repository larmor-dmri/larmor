About#

Larmor is written by Antonio Di Meglio. Its spherical deconvolution methods are reimplementations of those in MRIToolkit, by Alberto De Luca.

  • Citing says how to cite Larmor, and lists the original papers of each method.

  • Changelog says what changed in each version.

  • Roadmap says what comes next.

  • Contributing explains how to report a problem or propose a change.

Licence#

Larmor is free software, distributed under the GNU Lesser General Public License, version 3 only (LGPL-3.0-only), which builds on the GNU General Public License, version 3. The licence texts are in the files COPYING.LESSER and COPYING, in the repository and in every installed copy.

Parts of Larmor are reimplementations of methods in MRIToolkit (LGPL-3.0); the file NOTICE lists them. The compiled module includes Eigen (MPL-2.0), nanobind and pocketfft (both BSD 3-clause), unmodified.

Data credits#

Every brain image on this site is drawn from the ISMRM 2015 Tractography Challenge data, or from Larmor’s processing of it.

Maier-Hein KH, Neher PF, Houde JC, et al. The challenge of mapping the human connectome based on diffusion tractography. Nature Communications 8 (2017) 1349. doi:10.1038/s41467-017-01285-x. The data is published on Zenodo, doi:10.5281/zenodo.572345, under the CC BY 4.0 licence.

The other images are drawn from synthetic data made by the examples.
